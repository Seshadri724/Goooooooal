Controls

  • W A S D or arrows Move
  • Space / J Shoot when you have the ball, dash when you don't
  • 1–4 or click Pick a move in a duel
  • Space / click Skip a cutscene

Elements

A move with the element advantage gets ×1.2. A move that matches its user's own element gets ×1.1. Basic moves are neutral.

Duel math

score = (stat × 0.5 + move PWR + roll 0–24)
      × 1.10  move matches user's element
      × 1.20  element beats the other move

Dribble duel  Dribble  vs  Block
Shot block    shot     vs  Block
Keeper        shot × distance
              vs  30 + 34 + roll 0–24

Shot power falls off with distance: ×1.08 point blank, down to ×0.45 from your own half.
